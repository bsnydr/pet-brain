# Prep a vet visit

Goal: walk into the appointment with a short, factual brief so nothing is forgotten.

1. Read the recent entries from the store: the last few `Weight`, `Vet`, and `Behaviour` entries, plus the profile and any open `Task` items.
2. Produce a one-page brief with these sections:
   - **Since last visit:** weight trend (with dates), new behaviours or symptoms, anything that changed.
   - **Due now:** vaccinations, boosters, medications, or follow-ups that are due or overdue.
   - **Questions to ask:** drawn from recent behaviour notes and open tasks.
   - **Risks to raise:** breed-specific flags (for example, MDR1 drug sensitivity in at-risk herding breeds if the dog is untested), and any seasonal or regional risks for where the dog lives.
3. Keep it to facts the vet needs. Lead with anything time-sensitive.
4. After the visit, use the `log` skill to record what was decided (new meds, next due dates, weight taken).
